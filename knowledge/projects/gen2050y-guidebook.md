## Motivation

Dan created the GEN2050Y Mentoring Guidebook because prospective mentors of primary- and secondary-school students needed practical guidance beyond the course's theoretical Canvas modules and limited tutorial time.

## Guidebook

Dan compiled her team's mentoring experience into a centralized guide with practical tips and templates, including material that mentors could use when proposing and planning sessions.

The guidebook was built with MarkBind. Its source repository is https://github.com/DennieDan/guidebook.
