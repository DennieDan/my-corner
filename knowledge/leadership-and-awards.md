## PayPal x NUS Product Club Product LaunchPad 2026

Dan's team won first place out of six teams in the PayPal x NUS Product Club Product LaunchPad 2026. The program focused on product-management frameworks, industry mentorship, and a case study.

The team's case study addressed duplicated engineering work when onboarding new products. They proposed a scalable, multi-tenant onboarding platform built from reusable components and features for different users and product lines.

## UTown Residence leadership

Dan has served as Well-being Committee Leader in the UTown Residence Resident Council since August 2025. She led a five-person committee and worked on well-being campaigns, a sleep initiative, an aromatherapy workshop, and an appreciation dinner for more than 100 staff and student leaders.

Her reviewed résumé reports an average of 700 views per Telegram post for the sleep initiative and says 20% of surveyed residents reported a positive adjustment to their sleep habits.

## Other NUS leadership and volunteering

Dan served as a Public Relations Executive with the NUSSU Student Life Cell from January to August 2025. From April 2024 to April 2025, she was a Peer Connector and Video Production Leader with the NUS International Student Coordinating Office.
