## Hack & Roll 2026

Hack & Roll 2026 was Dan's first in-person hackathon. She built FinAunty, a personal budget-management application designed to reduce the effort of recording expenses.

The prototype let a user describe an expense to an AI assistant, which generated the corresponding SQL operation and stored the item. It also used optical character recognition so multiple items could be captured from a receipt. The project did not receive an award, but Dan has described it as an important lesson in rapid deployment and product differentiation.

## Capture the Flag activities

During the summer after her second year, Dan participated with a team in local Capture the Flag events, including BrainHack and Grey Cat the Flag.

Dan found the breadth of cybersecurity interesting, particularly its opportunities for exploration and learning. The experience also helped her recognize that she is more interested in artificial intelligence and database systems than in cybersecurity as a daily field of work. She remains interested in networking fundamentals, operating-system architecture, and database systems.
