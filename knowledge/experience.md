## Backend engineering internship

Dan completed a six-month Backend Engineering Internship at Cyber Sierra from June through December 2025. This was an internship, not a full-time role.

## AI agents and workflow automation

During the internship, Dan developed back-office automation and AI-agent workflows. She integrated Steampipe MCP and LangChain so natural-language requests could query databases across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.

Dan also built a Slack-based bot with LangGraph and Bolt that connected engineering updates with ticketing data and customer-facing Google Sheets. Another GitHub App watched pull-request status and helped synchronize ticket progress.

## Reporting and delivery work

Dan worked on a Python-based security report generator that produced formatted PDF reports from security-scanning results. She used document templates and LibreOffice-based processing for report formatting and table-of-contents updates.

Her résumé also records CI/CD work with Docker, Kubernetes, and GitHub Actions in support of microservices and a globally distributed engineering team.

## Go-to-market workflow

Dan designed a go-to-market workflow using n8n to coordinate Instantly, Apollo, and Freshsales. The workflow supported outreach before the Singapore FinTech Festival 2025 through email, LinkedIn, and calling steps connected by APIs and webhooks. She documented and handed over the working workflow at the end of her internship.

## Cross-functional and customer-facing work

Dan collaborated with Operations and Customer Success to understand workflow requirements and develop proofs of concept. She also represented Cyber Sierra at the Singapore FinTech Festival 2025, where she introduced Sierra AI's compliance and governance platform to visitors. She has described this as experience connecting technical product work with customer and business communication.
