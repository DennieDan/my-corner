## External contributor role

Dan contributed remotely to Tuturuuu from May through August 2025. Tuturuuu is an open-source productivity platform. Dan worked on Tuplan, its smart-calendar product, in a distributed team.

Tuturuuu was founded by Vo Hoang Phuc. Dan was referred to the company through a connection from her time at RMIT University Vietnam and was interviewed by him. His public LinkedIn profile is https://vn.linkedin.com/in/vohoangphuc.

## Calendar synchronization

Dan worked on active and background synchronization with Google Calendar. Active synchronization updated the calendar view while a user was online. Background synchronization used scheduled, queued, and deduplicated jobs to check for changes when users were offline.

She used React and Next.js for the active synchronization work and trigger.dev for asynchronous Google Calendar API workflows. Her reviewed extended résumé reports that the active synchronization work reduced event-display latency by 50% and that the background workflow supported high-frequency updates without blocking frontend performance.

## Teamwork and delivery

Dan worked with contributors across several countries and time zones. She proposed more frequent stand-ups when the team encountered synchronization and communication problems, helping contributors share progress and resolve blockers sooner.

The public project repository is https://github.com/tutur3u/platform.
