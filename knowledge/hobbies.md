## Inline skating

Dan began teaching herself inline skating during the summer after her first year at NUS. She practised using online videos and later joined NUS Skaters, where she learned from other skaters. She has described skating as a personal way to relax and challenge herself.

## Swimming

Dan learned swimming as a child and later joined the swimming co-curricular activity at Eusoff Hall. She trained independently alongside group practice.

Dan participated in the Inter-Hall Games in the women's individual 50-metre breaststroke and placed third in her heat. She did not win first place in the overall competition.
